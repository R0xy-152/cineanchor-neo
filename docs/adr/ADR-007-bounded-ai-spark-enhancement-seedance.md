# ADR-007: Bounded AI Spark Enhancement via Seedance (Deterministic Base + AI Aesthetic + Deterministic Text)

Date: 2026-06-29

Status: Accepted

Amends: `ADR-006` (which rejected AI for particles). This ADR carves a bounded exception for sparks/atmosphere only; `ADR-006`'s deterministic methods remain the fallback.

## Context

`ADR-006` mandated deterministic compositing for particles and explicitly rejected generative AI, because frame-to-frame consistency (the most critical of the four hard criteria) is exactly what AI is worst at for small/bright/fast particles. Two execution loops followed:

- Loop `03`: shipped but failed human acceptance (black ember layer, filter-wash look).
- Loop `04`: fixed visibility (embers now visible, non-black guard added) and passed the automated bar, but **failed the human aesthetic bar** — 启鸣's verdict: the embers are visible but the overall look (sparks, atmosphere, texture, light) does not reach the cinematic quality target of `ADR-005`. The blocker is *aesthetic ceiling*, not subject/camera (both confirmed correct).

This exhausted the deterministic-spark route at the *quality* level: the structure is right, the cinematic spark/atmosphere is not. Rather than re-open full generative AI (which `ADR-006` correctly rejected for subject-drift/flicker risk), Spike `05` tested a **bounded** approach (2b): keep everything structural deterministic, and let AI touch *only* the aesthetic layer of sparks/atmosphere, off of a clean deterministic base.

## Spike 05 result (the evidence for this ADR)

- **Setup:** Blender rendered a clean, text-free, particle-free structure base (subject + locked camera + lighting + occlusion + rim light + cloth anchor; `glints` disabled via the new flag). 启鸣 fed the base to Seedance (video-to-video reference + English prompt) externally; sparks/atmosphere were generated by AI; text is composited deterministically afterward (never through AI, to avoid letter garble).
- **Human acceptance (启鸣, 2026-06-29):** "明显观感更佳，无闪烁、无主体偏移." — noticeably better overall look; **no flicker** (acceptance #5, the make-or-break, passed) and **no subject drift** (acceptance #3 passed). All six acceptance criteria of `05-seedance-spike-requirements.md` met with no hard failure.

The single most-feared risks of the AI route — spark flicker and subject drift — did NOT materialize on this asset, because the deterministic base gave Seedance a strong structural anchor (rim + cloth + locked camera) to hold onto, and AI was confined to the aesthetic layer.

## Decision

**Adopt bounded AI spark/atmosphere enhancement as the aesthetic layer of route ①, via the following fixed pipeline:**

1. **Deterministic structure base (Blender).** Render subject + camera + lighting + occlusion + rim-light + cloth anchor, with NO particles, NO glints, NO text. Seedance-friendly format (1080p/720p, 24/30fps, H.264 MP4, 3–5s). This is the structural truth.
2. **AI aesthetic pass (Seedance, video-to-video).** Base used as a reference video + a prompt that (a) states what is already baked in and must be preserved (subject identity, camera motion, rim light, cloth), and (b) enhances atmosphere and **adds** sparks/embers — the only genuinely absent element. AI is confined to sparks + atmosphere/grade.
3. **Deterministic text re-composite.** Text/overlays are composited onto the AI output deterministically (FFmpeg), never sent through the AI.

**Hard boundaries (what AI may and may not touch):**
- AI MAY: sparks/embers form, atmospheric haze/depth, cinematic grade on top of existing rim/cloth.
- AI MUST NOT: define the subject, the camera path, or any text. These stay deterministic and are protected by the base + deterministic re-composite.

`ADR-006`'s deterministic particle Methods A/B are **retained as the fallback** for when Seedance is unavailable or fails on a given asset.

## Alternatives Considered

- **Stay fully deterministic (ADR-006 as-is).** Rejected at the quality level: loop 04 proved deterministic sparks reach a correct-but-not-cinematic ceiling for 启鸣's bar.
- **Full generative AI (no deterministic base).** Still rejected (per `ADR-006`/`ADR-005`): endangers subject identity and frame consistency. The whole point of 2b is to NOT do this.
- **Depth-map / control-first AI (ADR-005 route ④).** Deferred: higher engineering cost; 2b achieved the goal with a far lighter mechanism (reference video, no depth pipeline).

## Consequences

- **Quality:** unblocks the cinematic ceiling that deterministic sparks could not reach, while the two feared risks (flicker, subject drift) are held off by the deterministic base.
- **Dependency:** introduces an external dependency on Seedance (API access + cost) for the aesthetic layer. **Currently a manual step** — 启鸣 runs Seedance by hand; CC only outputs (base video, prompt). No automated API integration yet.
- **Generality is NOT yet proven.** This rests on a *single* spike (one asset + one prompt). Before any engineering investment, generality must be validated across multiple weapons/camera moves (next loop: semi-manual mass production). A single success may be asset/prompt luck.
- **Relationship to prior ADRs:** consistent with `ADR-005`'s ranking — AI stays out of the core control path (subject/camera/text) and is elevated only on the non-core aesthetic layer (route ② "commercial API polish", now made concrete for sparks/atmosphere). `ADR-004`'s no-full-frame-regeneration boundary is preserved (base is deterministic; AI enhances, does not regenerate structure).
- **Revert path:** if mass production shows 2b is unreliable across assets (flicker or drift appears on other weapons), fall back to `ADR-006` deterministic sparks and mark this ADR Superseded.

## 2026-06-29 Update — Generality confirmed (Loop 06)

Loop `06` (semi-manual mass production + parameter decoupling) was executed and **accepted by 启鸣 on 2026-06-29**. The 2b pipeline was exercised across **diverse assets and camera moves**, and the two highest-risk failure modes — **spark flicker and subject drift — did NOT recur**. This retires the "Generality is NOT yet proven" caveat in *Consequences* above, which rested on the single Spike-05 asset: 2b is now validated as **general**, not asset/prompt luck. The revert path in *Consequences* remains available should a future asset regress.
