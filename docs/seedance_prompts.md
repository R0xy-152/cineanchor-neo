## Base 1: Gold Arabesque — Dolly In

**File:** `storage/exports/mass_produce/gold_arabesque_dolly/final.mp4`
**Weapon:** AK-47 | Gold Arabesque skin (golden engraved arabesque pattern, warm metallic tones)
**Camera:** Dolly in (slow push toward the weapon, orthographic)

### Prompt (English — copy-paste into Seedance)

```
Reference video: a 4-second 1080p 24fps cinematic weapon showcase.

ALREADY BAKED IN (PRESERVE EXACTLY — DO NOT ALTER):
- Subject: AK-47 assault rifle with ornate gold arabesque engravings, warm brass/gold metallic finish. The weapon is the central hero asset — its shape, proportions, engravings, and metallic identity must stay pixel-perfect.
- Camera: slow orthographic dolly-in, pushing smoothly from a medium distance toward the weapon. The camera path must stay locked — no drift, no shake, no zoom deviation.
- Lighting: warm gold-tinted key light from front, amber rim light from left, warm coral rim light from right, neutral back light from above. Rim edges on the weapon body are visibly glowing with warm tones.
- Stage: dark floor plane, a faint glowing cyan ring on the floor beneath the weapon.
- Explicitly ABSENT: NO sparks, NO embers, NO fire particles, NO text overlay, NO HUD elements.

ENHANCE (WHAT IS ALREADY THERE):
- Deepen the cinematic atmosphere: add subtle volumetric haze around the weapon, increasing depth separation from the dark background.
- Amplify the warm rim lighting on the weapon edges — make the gold engravings catch light dramatically, with a polished, premium cinematic grade.
- Rich, high-contrast filmic look. The dark stage floor and the cyan ring should feel deeper and more atmospheric.

ADD (ONLY THE GENUINELY ABSENT — SPARKS / EMBERS):
- Floating golden/orange ember particles drifting upward from the floor around the weapon, small and numerous, like hot forge sparks cooling in air.
- Occasional bright gold light-streak sparks trailing diagonally across the frame, as if struck from metal.
- The sparks should feel organic and cinematic — not a particle system, but real fire embers caught in slow motion.
- Sparks must NOT obscure the weapon body. They stay in the foreground/background depth layers, decorating the space around the subject.
- Spark density: moderate — enough to feel alive and premium, not overwhelming or distracting.
```
