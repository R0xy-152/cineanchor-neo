# Route 3 Conservative AI Enhancement Report

Date: 2026-06-23

Branch: `spike/comfy-lut-enhance`

Status: PARTIAL

## Goal

Validate whether ComfyUI can be used as a color/style reference while FFmpeg applies stable full-video enhancement to the original rendered MP4.

This route does not replace final video frames with ComfyUI-generated frames.

## V0.1 Decision Logic

This report follows `CineAnchor_V0.1_开发决策文档.docx`: Route 3 is included in V0.1 only as conservative AI enhancement. Full-frame AI video regeneration is forbidden. ComfyUI may be used as keyframe color/style reference, but final video enhancement must remain FFmpeg/LUT/filter based.

Product-level wording for this result: Route 3 conservative AI enhancement validation.

## Why Route 3A Failed

Route 3A used direct full-frame SDXL img2img on rendered keyframes. The API and processing chain worked, but rendered subtitle text was corrupted in 4 of 4 reviewed `character_intro` frames. That makes direct full-frame img2img unacceptable for V0.1 text-bearing final renders.

## Why Direct Full-Frame AI Regeneration Is Excluded

- Frame-to-frame flicker risk is high when individual video frames are regenerated.
- Subject deformation risk is unacceptable for game character and product promotion clips.
- Output quality is unstable and difficult to guarantee across arbitrary frames.
- Processing 240 frames through ComfyUI can exceed 30 minutes.
- Previous direct full-frame img2img corrupted rendered subtitle text.

## Why Final Frames Are Not Replaced

ComfyUI output is treated as a color/style reference only. The final MP4 is generated by applying conservative FFmpeg filters to the original MP4, so rendered text and subject pixels are not regenerated by AI.

## Known Risks

- The color analysis is a bounded approximation, not a true LUT extraction pipeline.
- ComfyUI reference frames can still corrupt text; they must remain reference-only.
- Visual improvement is too mild to market as a core V0.1 AI feature.
- ComfyUI VRAM residency can conflict with Blender if both run at the same time.
- Manual visual review remains required before shipping any preset.

## Decision

Final Route 3 decision: PARTIAL.

The route is technically viable and stable: it processes only 4 keyframes per video through ComfyUI, derives conservative parameters, enhances the full video through FFmpeg, preserves text, preserves subject/product shape, and validates with ffprobe.

The visual improvement is not strong enough to make this a core V0.1 selling point. Recommendation for V0.1: keep this as an optional conservative enhancement preset, not a required pipeline step. V0.1 should continue to exclude full-frame AI video regeneration.
